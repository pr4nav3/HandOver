# HandOver mark

The mark is an **H whose crossbar becomes a forward handoff arrow**. It represents knowledge moving from the
expert to the learner without relying on a camera, sparkle, brain, or other generic AI symbol.

- `handover-mark.svg` is the canonical monochrome mark. Tint it with `currentColor`.
- `handover-favicon.svg` is the compact blue-and-white container for browser and launcher contexts.
- Keep the mark upright, use one flat color, and leave at least one stroke width of clear space around it.
- Do not add gradients, shadows, outlines, or separate colors to the arrow.

The 64-unit construction uses an 8-unit rounded stroke and was checked at 16 px. Apple app-icon PNGs are
mechanically rendered from `apps/apple/Resources/Brand/HandOverAppIcon.svg`; the SVG remains the editable source.
