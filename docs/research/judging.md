## 4 minute demo
- 0:00 to 0:25 hook: "Experts teach the same task 50 times. Here the expert does it once."
- 0:25 to 1:05 teach screen, bike timeline, click a clip. Wi-Fi fails: all pre-cut on disk. Gemini fails: show last night's lesson.json and say so.
- 1:05 to 2:50 judge folds under camera. Glasses fail: chest iPhone capture page (practice swap under 10 s). Wi-Fi fails: hotspot. API down: replay mode, say "this is a recording". Misfire: "Next always works". Step 5 flip catch is the moment. Judge declines: teammate folds; backup paper cup.
- 2:50 to 3:20 every OS beat: same lesson open on iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows side by side, advance once, all update together. Only devices actually shown. HEVC normalized to H.264 so clips play on Windows. Voice next is Chrome only unless tested.
